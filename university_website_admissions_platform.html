Our Foundation

Institutional History & Vision

Established to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical industry application, Crown Heights University stands as a beacon of academic distinction. Fully accredited by the National Universities Commission (NUC), CHU offers standard degree programs designed to meet international benchmarks.

Our green campus incorporates modern architectural designs with eco-friendly infrastructure, providing students with an inspiring academic sanctuary.

Our Mission

To provide world-class holistic education, encouraging critical inquiry, ethical responsibility, and technological innovation for societal advancement.

UTME / 100 Level Entry

Direct Entry / 200 Level

ND (Upper Credit), HND, or JUPEB/A-Level qualifications in relevant subject combinations.
